Chicago Design Week brought together thousands of people from across the world to explore new ideas shaping the future of work – and this year, Steelcase and its community of brands were recognized with several industry awards for design excellence. The energy extended across Design Week at Fulton Market Design Days and the Merchandise Mart, where Steelcase and partner brand showrooms created a connected experience that showcased how thoughtful design can help people think better.
